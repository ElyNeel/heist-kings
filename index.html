On the job
    The lock-up is the lobby. Walk about, have a go on the bag, the hoop and the darts, or sit on the sofa next to a mate and play Kick-Off on the telly. Tab brings this back up.

    How it works. You're doing the job together. One of you is secretly the snitch and only they know.

    The snitch has a burner phone: they can text the police, swap gold for fakes (fakes go plink when knocked) and drop a buzzing phone that brings the police quicker. They'll also "accidentally" drop things.

    Watch out for the guard. The second time he gets you, he locks you in the bathroom. A mate can let you out (hold E on the bathroom door). While you wait, you watch the house cameras and shout directions.

    Press X to go floppy, and a mate can pick you up and carry you. Or throw you. Whisper and only your mates hear you; shout and the whole house wakes up.

    Heavy stuff needs two of you. When you're done, anyone can start the vote at the van: pick who to leave behind. Leave the snitch and get £30,000 of real gold, and you're away. Get it wrong and the van gets pulled over on the motorway.
